## Vedic Mathematics

This repository explores the principles and practical applications of Vedic Mathematics, including the 16 Sutras and 13 Sub-Sutras.

The focus is on understanding mathematical concepts, developing mental calculation skills, recognizing patterns and applying systematic problem-solving techniques.

## 16 Vedic Sutras

The 16 Sutras form the core principles of Vedic Mathematics and will be studied with their mathematical applications and examples.

## 13 Sub-Sutras

The 13 Sub-Sutras provide additional methods and techniques that complement the main Sutras and will be explored through practical mathematical problems.
